# "Neighbors" — series bible (read by the writer and critic agents)

## Premise
Two animal families live in two small houses separated by a low wooden fence.
The cats are cunning, lazy and proud; the dogs are loud, kind and naive.
Animals live on their own in a human-like everyday world (kitchen, fridge,
phone, grill, garden hose, robot vacuum). No humans ever appear.

## Format
- Vertical short, 15–30 seconds, understandable with no words.
- No human speech: meows, barks, sound effects and light music only.
- Structure: hook in the first 3 seconds -> escalation -> surprise twist ->
  a reaction shot at the very end.
- Every episode is a NEW everyday situation. Never repeat a past plot.

## Cat family
- Boris (dad): large chubby orange tabby, lazy strategist, loves food and his
  armchair, always hiding something from his wife. Signature: slowly backs out
  of frame when caught.
- Mia (mom): elegant white long-haired cat, strict, notices everything.
  Signature: silently raises one eyebrow.
- Pixel (kitten): tiny orange-and-white kitten, curious, brave in theory,
  best friend of puppy Bobo. Signature: puffs up and hops sideways.

## Dog family
- Rex (dad): big golden retriever, red bandana, "I'll fix it myself", fixes
  nothing. Signature: happily wags his tail in the middle of a disaster.
- Daisy (mom): ginger corgi with a yellow bow, energetic organizer, best
  friend of Mia. Signature: stamps her paw and stares.
- Bobo (puppy): fluffy golden puppy with short corgi legs, clumsy, loyal,
  best friend of Pixel. Signature: zoomies in circles.

## Relationships
- Pixel and Bobo: best friends, secretly from their dads.
- Boris and Rex: over-the-fence rivals (barbecue, sunny spot, whose yard is better).
- Mia and Daisy: friends and allies; together they expose their husbands,
  usually in the final shot ("the double eyebrow").
- Boris and Bobo: grump and pest — Bobo's red ball keeps landing on sleeping Boris.

## Recurring elements
- Bobo's red ball flies over the fence.
- The hole in the fence is a portal for the kids; dads try to patch it.
- Final shot of Mia and Daisy looking at their guilty husbands.

## Branch balance
Out of every 10 episodes: about 3 cats, 3 dogs, 4 crossover.

## Hard rules
- Family-friendly humor for a general audience 13+: everyday situations
  (diet, repairs, delivery, chores, weather, holidays, neighbor rivalry).
- No romance drama, cheating, pregnancy, violence, injury, gore, fear or
  danger to the kittens and puppies, no toilet humor.
- No humans, no real brands, no logos, no famous characters or movies.
- Never frame the video as real footage (no "security camera, this is real").
